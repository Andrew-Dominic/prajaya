Join as Volunteer

Serve. Learn. Lead. Create Impact.

PRAJAYA Foundation welcomes responsible and committed individuals who wish to contribute their time, knowledge, skills, and energy towards community development.

Our volunteers support programmes relating to education, health, legal awareness, youth development, women empowerment, environmental protection, sports, social welfare, skill development, research, awareness campaigns, and community service.

Volunteering with PRAJAYA Foundation is an opportunity to serve society, develop leadership skills, gain meaningful experience, and work with people who believe in creating positive and sustainable change.

Who Can Volunteer?

Students, professionals, teachers, trainers, lawyers, healthcare workers, social workers, artists, sportspersons, retired persons, homemakers, and other socially responsible individuals may apply.

Applicants below 18 years of age must submit the consent of their parent or legal guardian.

Registration does not automatically guarantee selection or assignment. Volunteer opportunities are provided based on Foundation requirements, suitability, availability, conduct, skills, and programme needs.

Ways to Volunteer

You may volunteer in the following areas:

  • Education and tutoring
  • Computer and digital literacy
  • Skill development
  • Legal literacy and awareness
  • Health and wellness programmes
  • Youth leadership
  • Women empowerment
  • Child development
  • Senior citizen support
  • Disability inclusion
  • Environmental activities
  • Disaster relief and community support
  • Sports, fitness, and karate
  • Event management
  • Research and documentation
  • Photography and videography
  • Graphic design
  • Content writing
  • Social media support
  • Fundraising support
  • Administration and coordination
  • Professional and technical services

Volunteer Process

  1. Complete the online volunteer registration form.
  2. Accept the Volunteer Terms & Conditions.
  3. Agree to the Volunteer Code of Conduct.
  4. Submit the required information and documents.
  5. Attend an interaction, verification, or orientation session where required.
  6. Receive an assignment based on suitability and programme availability.
  7. Complete the assigned service responsibly.
  8. Submit reports, attendance, photographs, or feedback where required.

Important Information

Volunteering is voluntary and does not create an employer-employee relationship with PRAJAYA Foundation.

Volunteers are not authorised to collect money, receive donations, issue receipts, sign documents, make commitments, speak to the media, or represent PRAJAYA Foundation unless expressly authorised in writing.

Certificates, appreciation letters, experience letters, and recommendations may be issued based on actual contribution, attendance, conduct, completion of responsibilities, and Foundation policy.
